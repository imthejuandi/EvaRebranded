# EVA orchestration playbook

Use this when a material website idea spans specialties or needs independent judgment. For narrow routine work, use the same gates in a short brief. If delegation or a model/skill is unavailable, say how coverage was obtained or what remains unverified; never manufacture independence or a pass.

## Chief of Staff handoff

Write a one-page brief before spawning implementation roles:

- **Decision and scope:** One customer question; the evidence that it currently fails; exact branch/URL and route; latest owner direction; in and out; proposed versus approved versus unknown; who can decide a product or clinical question.
- **Impact map:** Mark each of copy, explanation, ES/EN, design, code, animation, accessibility, claims, performance and routing as changed, regression-only or out. A protected component needs a test, not necessarily an agent. Start with the smallest candidate likely to pass.
- **Assignments:** One sentence per role naming its deliverable, exact source/artifact and no-touch boundaries, available model/effort, named skills, dependencies, evidence, and stop/escalation condition. Each output separates facts, recommendations, uncertainties, and blocking issues; ask for reproducible evidence, not long internal reasoning.
- **Gates:** Define pass/fail before implementation. Choose cheap deterministic tests first. Use a fresh judge for subjective judgment on the actual integrated render; a plan critique is not final QA. Budget up to two targeted correction rounds before reassessing scope, unless the owner requests another cadence. No arbitrary scores or reviewer quotas.

Suggested routing, not mandatory configuration: Astra high for ambiguous planning or strong visual judgment; Astra medium for a fresh integrative judge; Sol medium for scoped copy variants, structured review or implementation; Sol high when code complexity warrants it; Sol low for repeatable evidence checks. Respect explicit model assignments, such as Astra for design only on the earlier statement piece. Use the available wellness marketing, UX copy and brand skills; Frontend Design for implementation and Impeccable for typography/reflow when available. Use primary evidence and qualified clinical review for new medical claims, not automated consensus as sign-off.

## Plan review and integration

The second agent gets the proposed plan, owner constraints and baseline evidence, not a direction to agree. It returns only higher-impact omissions, unnecessary roles, dependency/conflict risks, or tests that fail to establish the outcome. The Chief of Staff accepts or rejects each material critique with a brief reason and freezes the brief. Do not give separate agents simultaneous write ownership of overlapping files. Parallelize independent checks or proposals; share agreed copy/data with downstream design and implementation. Use one integrator and have it compare the integrated diff with non-goals.

For comprehension, collect unprimed observations from matched captures before exposing the rubric, expected answer or author verdicts. Then score those observations. The technical judge may receive the explicit rubric from the outset. Log build, screenshots and assertions from the exact revision. For each failed criterion, require an observed issue, severity and smallest fix. Fix blockers, rerender and retest relevant conditions. Extra polish is optional, and genuine product uncertainty goes back to Juan Diego. Update the decision ledger with rejected options and reasons supported by actual use. Keep tactical prompt improvements separate from product decisions.

## Worked example: a plain-language problem before Alex

- **Scope:** Existing rebrand review v70, first post-hero stretch. Confirm where the problem appears now and where Alex lands; do not inherit the isolated entry experiment. Introduce the practical difficulty of making sense of lab numbers, then let the existing fictional Alex example demonstrate one benefit. Do not change hero, runner, logo, CTAs or their destinations; no new health statistic, feature claim or medical threshold.
- **Strategy:** Chief of Staff drafts scope and witnesses. Plan checker should remove standalone animation, new-media and medical-writing work unless a genuine change is detected. Wellness/UX copy owns two short options in idiomatic ES and EN and their intended takeaway. Frontend/brand owner checks site placement and hierarchy, then integrates the selected one in the existing component system. If typography or mobile reflow needs special attention, use Impeccable within the visual pass; no automatic extra agent. Actual clinical evidence review is conditional on adding or changing a clinical claim.
- **Evidence:** On ES/EN narrow mobile and desktop, the problem is visible in the narrative before Alex, Alex before kit details, the existing example is visibly fictional, and the original hero is unchanged. Confirm one Alex example (not duplicate sections), same hero links and reveal, and readable wrapping. On matched captures, first ask a fresh reader what they took away, what on the page supports that, and what they had to guess. Only then apply the expected takeaway. If both versions are equally clear, record that no improvement was shown. An agent reading the preview is not a human usability study. No deploy to production.

## Stress test: claim or scope conflict

For ‘make kit copy more authoritative, feature ApoB, and tell everyone what to aim for’, distinguish hierarchy, implied panel composition, and medical claims. ApoB applies to enhanced at-home, not standard; full-panel scope is unknown. No universal target. Hold unsupported claims if qualified review is unavailable; continue unrelated safe changes. If the owner supersedes the scope, invalidate affected work and verdicts only, and preserve the approved baseline.
