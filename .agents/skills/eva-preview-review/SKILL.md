# EVA Preview Review

Act as Chief of Staff for **one authorized EVA customer outcome**. Use the existing rebrand, its [decision ledger](references/decision-ledger.md) and project evidence. Do not treat the latest experimental preview as the approved baseline. Use the [orchestration playbook](references/orchestration.md) for role, model and skill routing and fixtures.

**Establish the run.** Record the owner's current intent, action allowed, baseline route and exact revision, skill revision, observed gap and protected behavior. Define a customer-level success criterion, deterministic checks, and what would justify no change. Recheck authorization before dispatch and delivery. If instructions or the candidate change, invalidate affected assignments and verdicts; preserve unaffected evidence.

**Design and challenge the plan.** Mark components as changed, regression-only or out. For each necessary workstream name the owner, actual available model/effort and skills, exact input, scope, deliverable, dependency and acceptance test. Give material plans to a separate reviewer to identify the most consequential missing check or redundant work. Record material changes or reasons for declining them. Preserve exact dispatched prompts for process pilots.

**Execute and integrate.** Parallelize only independent work; one integrator owns the final diff. Separate confirmed product facts, scientific evidence, preferences and unknowns. Verify implied claims from layout, adjacency and translation as well as text. Do not invent scope, eligibility, availability or clinical sign-off. If review is unavailable, hold or omit the affected claim and continue unaffected work. Use fictional fixtures, never real health data.

**Verify the exact output.** Check scoped diff and deterministic assertions against the pinned candidate; exercise affected ES/EN routes and relevant screen/motion states. For comprehension, collect observations from a fresh reader before revealing the brief, expected takeaway or author verdicts. Ask what the reader understood, what on screen supports it, and what they had to guess. Use matched baseline and candidate when claiming improvement; mask labels/order where practical. Only then score each material criterion pass, fail, or unverified with inspectable evidence. An unavailable reviewer is not an independent pass, and an AI assessment is not customer evidence. Fix the smallest blocker, rerender and retest; reopen the brief on scope change or repeated failure. Preview approval is not release approval.

**Return a decision, not ceremony.** Deliver the exact preview and revisions, before/after evidence, frozen prompts where requested, actual diff, first/final verdicts, limits and any owner decision. For a process pilot, commission an independent retrospective: what changed the outcome, what was redundant, and the one workflow adjustment evidence supports. Record owner decisions and rejected alternatives in the ledger; promote transferable lessons, not one-off preferences.
