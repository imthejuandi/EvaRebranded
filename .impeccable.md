## Design Context

### Users
Spanish-speaking adults exploring preventative health as a positive lifestyle choice. They need to understand EVA’s at-home biomarker service and decide whether to request their first kit. This is the separate design-study landing, not the production app.

### Brand Personality
Confident, kinetic, precise. These words synthesize the creator’s repeated direction: health should feel exciting, calm and aspirational, with clear information and no anxiety-led selling.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the creator-approved dark ink canvas, soft-glow running imagery, analog dotted lettering and warm paper/amber palette. Sofi Health, Clearwater London and the shared SAVEE board are the established references. Preserve the official EVA logo. Avoid generic component grids, floral hero imagery, distorted letters, and competing decorative effects.

### Design Principles
1. Compose around the human subject. Keep the runner’s face unobstructed and preserve her natural rightward exit.
2. Conoce tu remains above and to the left of salud. Balance visual weight; do not force equal dimensions or deform the glyphs.
3. Group related information and action. Use a consistent alignment grid and generous, deliberate separation between unrelated elements.
4. Keep the first-kit CTA compact with its approved invitation, detail and price. The headline and runner lead; navigation and controls remain secondary.
5. Adapt mobile placement, preserve readable type and touch targets, and keep static/reduced-motion views consistent. Retain all previous source versions.

The latest explicit request authorizes Impeccable for this refinement and supersedes the prior restriction against using it. Existing palette and media choices take precedence over generic skill defaults.

### Typeface selection
Brand words: confident, kinetic, precise. The physical reference is a premium performance-apparel label, not a clinical dashboard. Reflex candidates Inter, Instrument Sans, and IBM Plex Sans were rejected. Google Fonts specimens considered: Hanken Grotesk, Albert Sans, and Onest. Choose Hanken Grotesk for the hero’s solid text: open, human forms that sit quietly beside the distinctive analog serif. Preserve all logo and analog artwork.
