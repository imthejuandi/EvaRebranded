# Tasso homepage film: One small step

Creative script, loop 1. This is the product scene already inside the homepage scroll film, not an additional feature carousel. Research is in `tasso-process-research.md`. The current EVA business journey is supplied by the owner: kit at home, Tasso collection, prepaid return, results in two working days after laboratory receipt.

## The idea

A small object. A short journey. A more useful understanding of your health.

Keep the large **One small step.** / **Un pequeño gesto.** identity and EVA’s paper, ink, coral and quiet photographic light. Give that promise a concrete sequence: **Collect at home → Return the sample → Understand your results**. Each action has one visual subject, one short headline and one plain explanation. The sequence is cinematic through framing, continuity and restraint. It does not need generated hands, a fictional device demonstration, busy infographic arrows or a spinning 3D product.

Use the verified manufacturer product photograph, the retained real in-use photograph, and authored, clearly illustrative return/report objects. The exact original URL of the retained in-use asset is unverified; its placement has been checked against the manufacturer instructions. The final business benefit is understanding, not a medical claim. Neither the sample nor the device transforms into a report.

## Position and composition

- Replace the static content of `EvaFilm`’s existing `product-scene`. Continue to use the same scroll clock and scene movement. Do not add an independent autoplay clock or extend the entire homepage just to make these three beats.
- The parent product scene enters during frames 130–280 and begins giving way to the amber insight scene at 465. Three fully framed beats live within 280–465.
- Desktop: retained editorial heading in the left upper area, active step copy beneath it, one large visual on the right. Leave space between text and art. A fine three-part index provides orientation, never three boxed cards.
- Phone: compact common heading, art above active step text, same three-step index. Protect all text from the existing bottom navigation. On short screens reduce art height first; essential body copy and timing qualifier may not disappear.
- Warm paper stays continuous through all three shots. The scene exits with the existing upward translation into the amber biomarker chapter. Avoid a new colored background or dramatic lighting switch per step.

## Asset and motion rules

1. **Product and use:** `/art/tasso-plus-product.jpg` plus the retained real in-use photograph. The research record distinguishes the verified product source from the in-use asset's unverified original URL. The existing glow still may support the opening visual identity, but the real in-use photography demonstrates placement. Do not use the generated home-ritual image as evidence for physical use.
2. **Return:** authored SVG/CSS/React objects with material-like paper and a protected sample. This is an illustration of packing order, not a manufacturer packaging reproduction. Never mail the whole Tasso device in the animation. Do not show loose blood or an uncapped tube inside an envelope.
3. **Results:** a typographic illustration of interpretation. It may use biomarker names but no fabricated values or personal outcomes. No generic dashboard mockup, neon pulse, glass panel, decorative particles or AI-generated medical UI.
4. **Motion:** native scroll progress; opacity and translate only for photograph/type, with restrained flap rotation permitted for the paper envelope. Scene movement stays with the existing parent. No idle loop is necessary. Stopped scroll means a stable readable composition.
5. **Type:** use the existing Hanken/serif pairing. Headlines are simple and concrete. Use sufficient contrast on paper; tiny pale captions undermine the ease promise.
6. **Semantics:** the film is currently `aria-hidden`. Provide one shared semantic ordered-list transcript outside that hidden subtree. With reduced motion, expose the three steps as a visible, fully readable static sequence, including laboratory-receipt timing and the real upper-arm photograph. Essential content cannot depend on motion, mouse hovering, or successful video playback.

## Production review questions

- Can a viewer tell where the kit arrives, what is collected, what is returned, and when results follow?
- Is one press and release clear, with relaxed arm/tube down, and no suggestion of instant collection?
- Does the return image show a sealed labeled sample inside protection before the prepaid envelope?
- Is the two-working-day timing explicitly anchored to lab receipt on screen, including phone layouts?
- Does every phase have a stable readable hold, with no simultaneous overlapping copy?
- Does the result scene promise understanding without inventing test results or outcomes?
- Do desktop, compact phone, reverse scroll, reduced-motion and screen-reader paths convey the same story?

The judge owns the acceptance decision. Corrections route to research for facts, creative for meaning/choreography, or executor for implementation, for a maximum of three total review loops.
