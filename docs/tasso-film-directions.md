# Tasso film comparison: original plus three new directions

Creative round 2, following feedback that the first React treatment felt too basic. The original stays available as option 0. The three new options use newly generated photographs converted to actual image-to-video clips: three shots of approximately five seconds per option. React supplies playback, comparison and readable bilingual copy; it does not stand in for the film motion.

Factual source: `tasso-process-research.md`. The longer process/IFU companion copy remains in `tasso-cinematic-script.md`. These films explain a service, not the complete device instructions.

## Common production specification

- Deliver three 16:9, approximately five-second clips per direction, about 15 seconds assembled. Preserve each original generated still as the poster. Native resolution should be at least 1280×720 when supported; choose consistent resolution and frame rate across a direction.
- Generate a coherent still for each shot first, inspect product and hand geometry, then animate that still through I2V. Do not fix a malformed device by hiding it with motion.
- Use each direction's first generated still as an additional continuity reference for its later stills: preserve material/light in A and C, and the same adult, room and table in B. B3 is a later results-reading moment, not the collection session: remove the Tasso device, tube and test kit from the person/table. Never carry the in-use device into a results shot merely to preserve continuity.
- Every device shot uses `/workspace/eva-content-review/public/art/tasso-plus-product.jpg` as the exact identity reference and `/workspace/eva-content-review/public/art/tasso-plus-in-use.png` as a placement/orientation reference. White oval housing, coral circular button, embossed mark, lower connector, believable size. On the outer upper arm with the attached tube below it, pointing down. Do not mirror the device or invent extra ports.
- The camera, natural light, real adult breathing, paper and hands may move. The device must keep its shape and stay attached. Collection shots begin with correct placement already established. No generated press/depression mechanism, extra finger, needle, repeated pressing, blood fill or unrealistic instantaneous collection. The separate caption tells the viewer to press once and release.
- The adult is seated for collection, including tightly framed A1 and C1 shots where the chair may stay outside the crop. Keep the arm relaxed down; do not imply collection while standing, walking or training.
- The return shot shows a detached, capped, labeled sample inside a closed, sealed protective supplied carrier, then a prepaid return envelope. The opaque carrier may obscure the tube; it must never imply that a loose tube or the used device is posted. No invented courier, clinical accreditation, regulatory mark or tracking identifier.
- Results scenes show authentic human attention and/or a physical editorial report. No invented biomarker values, diagnoses, biological age or score. Any screen/report layout is an illustrative surface; actual wording is rendered separately by the website.
- No text baked into generated imagery, no logos invented by the image model, no subtitles, no sound-dependent instructions. Reserve clean negative space for the web copy. No neon, particles, holograms, floating device, impossible depth-of-field, plastic skin, aggressive lens flare or glossy science-fiction dashboards.
- Prompt assembly: each shot's image prompt below is used with these common constraints and, for device shots, both reference images. Each I2V prompt uses its inspected generated still as the start image. Image fidelity takes precedence over complex action.

## A — Studio Precision / Precisión de estudio

Pitch: **The process, in its simplest form.** / **El proceso, en su forma más sencilla.**

A warm ivory world. Real materials. Exact product geometry. The visual pleasure comes from close focus, soft raking light and decisive framing. Each shot shares the same ivory surface, quiet ink detail and coral accent; the scene feels like one carefully photographed product film.

### A1 · The collection, 0–5s

**Image prompt:** Premium editorial product photograph on a warm ivory seamless set. Tight three-quarter close-up of a real adult's relaxed outer upper arm, naturally textured skin, with the exact referenced Tasso+ device correctly adhered to the upper arm. The white oval shell and coral circular button are crisp and unchanged; the compatible collection tube is attached below and points straight down. Arm relaxed, never flexed or raised. A small opened unbranded home-test kit rests softly out of focus on the ivory surface below, establishing delivery without competing with the device. Frame the arm and tube entirely, no face needed. Large diffuse daylight key from upper left, delicate contact shadows, natural photographic depth of field, restrained color. The device is real medical hardware, not a jewel or rendered toy. No visible blood, no hand pressing the device, no readable text. Leave the leftmost third quiet for copy.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second continuous macro dolly, camera moves slowly 3cm laterally and a tiny amount closer toward the correctly attached device. Natural subtle breathing in the adult's relaxed arm only. Soft light rolls gently across the white shell and coral button. Keep the exact device shape, skin attachment, downward tube and tube connection fixed. No touch, no button movement, no blood filling, no new objects, no cut, no text. Finish with a still readable hero frame.

### A2 · The return, 5–10s

**Image prompt:** Tactile studio still life on the same warm ivory seamless table. A small capped and labeled sample is safely contained inside a closed, sealed protective sample carrier, with an opaque protective panel covering the tube body and at most a small clear window showing the capped sample. The sealed carrier sits just inside the mouth of a larger off-white prepaid return envelope; its packing order is immediately clear. One real adult hand holds only the outer carrier edge. The same warm diffuse side light reveals paper grain, folded edges and a modest soft shadow. Minimal ink-colored label placeholders, no readable medical details, no courier logos or invented regulatory symbols. Do not show the Tasso device. Frame close enough to understand the protective carrier and envelope as distinct objects; leave a quiet upper-left area for website copy.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second close-up with a locked camera and a gentle 2cm push-in. The hand slowly settles the protective carrier a short distance farther into the return envelope, then releases it. The capped sample stays fully protected and never moves independently. The envelope flap settles a little under gravity; no dramatic origami, no unsupported flying paper, no extra fingers, no tube handling or device. Keep all labels unchanging and unreadable. End on the protected, ready-to-return envelope.

### A3 · The understanding, 10–15s

**Image prompt:** Warm ivory studio photograph of a physical, elegantly typeset health report sheet on the same paper surface, at a subtle oblique angle. The sheet has three clean horizontal sections and restrained ink rules, with small blank label spaces and softly visible short lines suggesting explanation, context and trends; no readable text, numerical results or medical verdicts. A coral tab is the only color accent. Soft raking daylight and tactile paper grain. One adult fingertip rests calmly at the outside lower edge, conveying attention rather than a diagnostic action. Crisp, realistic paper edges and unhurried photographic simplicity. Leave generous negative space beside the report for the website's two-working-day timing.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second slow overhead camera drift, 3cm forward. Focus moves gently from the report's near paper edge to its three calm explanatory sections, then settles. A slight natural adjustment of the resting hand only. The sheet does not generate new text, values, charts or symbols. No glowing screen, no data particles, no diagram morph, no page flip, no cut. End in a crisp quiet frame suitable for the results caption.

## B — Morning at Home / Una mañana en casa

Pitch: **A small moment in an ordinary day.** / **Un pequeño momento en un día cualquiera.**

A credible adult morning, photographed at home. This direction sells ease through familiar surroundings and unperformed behavior. Keep one person, one kitchen table, one soft window-light direction and ordinary clothes through all three clips. Avoid the exaggerated smile and immaculate showroom kitchen typical of generic wellness advertising.

### B1 · A moment in the day, 0–5s

**Image prompt:** Quiet documentary-style cinematic photograph of an adult aged about 35–50 seated comfortably at their own modest kitchen table in soft early daylight. Sleeveless neutral cotton top, relaxed outer upper arm visible, with the exact referenced Tasso+ already correctly attached and its connected tube pointing down. Natural skin and believable body proportions, no glamorous pose. A small opened home-test kit with simple protective contents sits on the table, alongside an ordinary glass of water; no food interacting with the test. The adult looks calmly toward the window rather than performing for the camera. Warm wood, soft linen, real lived-in details with restraint. Device and tube remain readable at this medium-close framing. No blood or pressing action. Leave calm wall/window space on the left for copy.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second natural handheld-but-steady cinematic move, a gentle 4cm lateral drift at seated eye level. The adult breathes naturally and makes a very small gaze shift toward the window; their collection arm remains relaxed and still with tube down. Keep the Tasso hardware completely stable and identical to the reference. Daylight changes imperceptibly across the table. No extra hands, no collection action, no press, no filling tube, no smile-to-camera, no cut or text.

### B2 · Ready to return, 5–10s

**Image prompt:** Close documentary photograph on the same real kitchen table in the same warm morning light. The same adult's hands work only on the outside of a supplied protective sample carrier and a larger plain prepaid return envelope. The capped, labeled sample is already protected inside the carrier; show a small protected glimpse or label panel, never a loose uncapped tube. One hand steadies the envelope while the other lightly holds the carrier at the opening. A simple kit instruction sheet is out of focus nearby, without readable text. Natural fingernails, ordinary hands, believable paper texture. No Tasso device, no courier logo, no exact mailing-address data, no excessive props. Frame tightly on the practical, easy action.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second single take. The adult slowly slides the protected sample carrier about 2cm into the prepaid envelope while the other hand steadies it. Pause and let the outer envelope flap lower naturally. Camera makes only a tiny forward move; keep hand anatomy, paper edges and carrier geometry stable. No opening or pouring the sample, no bare tube, no device mailing, no magically appearing labels, no extra fingers or cut. End on the envelope resting on the table, ready for return.

### B3 · Make sense of it, 10–15s

**Image prompt:** Cinematic over-the-shoulder photograph of the same adult at the same kitchen table in gentle window light, now calmly reading their health insights on a laptop or tablet on a later day. There is no Tasso device or tube on the arm and no test kit on the table. The screen is angled realistically and shows a restrained neutral report layout with three simple explanatory sections and small trend-like lines, but no legible text, numbers, diagnoses, scores or personal identity. Natural hand on the table, thoughtful relaxed posture, no exaggerated celebration or medical clothing. The visual benefit is attention and understanding. Keep the screen modest in the composition and leave quiet room beside it for a sharp website-rendered timing caption.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second slow shoulder-level push toward the adult and report screen. The adult makes a small natural head movement while reading, one finger may scroll a short distance with no changing or generating detailed content. Preserve screen geometry and stable report-like forms; avoid gibberish text motion. Daylight and human skin stay natural. No smile performance, notification popups, diagnostic badge, chart morph, generated numbers or cut. Finish on a calm reading moment.

## C — Light into Insight / De la luz a la claridad

Pitch: **A close look. A clearer understanding.** / **Una mirada cercana. Una visión más clara.**

An editorial film in EVA ink and amber. Real materials emerge from shadow through controlled photographic lighting. The drama lives in a small movement of light and focus, not in fantasy anatomy, luminous blood or a futuristic laboratory. Coral remains the device's real button color; amber comes from the set lighting.

### C1 · A small point of contact, 0–5s

**Image prompt:** Photoreal cinematic super-close product-and-skin photograph against deep EVA ink black, with a narrow warm amber sidelight and a soft neutral fill. The exact referenced Tasso+ is correctly adhered to the outer upper arm of a real adult; its white shell, coral button, embossed mark and downward attached tube remain physically correct. Include enough of the relaxed arm to make placement unambiguous; do not reduce the body to an abstract unidentified skin patch. Honest pores and soft natural texture, no synthetic sheen. The arm is still and relaxed. Device crisp, tube visible and down. Large quiet ink negative space on the left for copy. No glow within the body, no visible veins, no needle, no blood and no pressing hand.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second controlled macro slider move, about 3cm across the device, with a very slow warm sidelight sweep across its white housing. Tiny natural breathing only; device attachment and downward tube are fixed. Let focus settle from the near shell edge onto the coral button, without changing its geometry or depressing it. No blood movement, particles, neon, skin morph, floating device, new object or cut. Finish with clear placement and realistic tactile light.

### C2 · A protected journey, 5–10s

**Image prompt:** Low-key editorial photograph of a plain paper prepaid return envelope and a distinct protective sample carrier on a dark ink-colored tabletop. A capped, labeled sample is already safely inside the carrier, with its body obscured by protection. The carrier rests partly inside the envelope, making the packing order unmistakable. A warm amber strip of real studio light reveals paper edges, folds and a small restrained label area; neutral fill keeps all shapes understandable. No device, no blood, no courier brand, no regulatory symbols, no generated readable wording. Minimal scene, believable gravity, tactile paper rather than glossy black luxury packaging. Leave space above for website copy.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second slow diagonal camera move of 3cm while a warm light edge travels gently across the protective carrier and envelope. The objects remain grounded and already packed; the outer envelope flap may settle slightly under gravity but must not fold itself closed magically. Keep sample fully protected and sealed, no independent tube motion, no Tasso device, no hand invention, no floating packaging, no text change, no particle trails or cut. End with the packing structure clearly readable.

### C3 · Clarity, 10–15s

**Image prompt:** Cinematic editorial photograph of a single warm-white health report sheet on a deep ink tabletop, revealed by soft amber side light and a quiet neutral fill. The report has three restrained explanatory areas with thin ink rules and generous spacing, no legible text, no numerical measurements or diagnosis. One small coral edge tab echoes the Tasso button without resembling the device. The paper is real, tactile and still, with a softly lit upper edge and grounded shadow. A second sheet is barely visible beneath it to suggest continuity over time without decorative chart clutter. Large empty ink space remains for the website's results title and laboratory-receipt timing. No screen, hologram, luminous veins, floating information, glossy sci-fi UI or magical transformation.

**I2V prompt:** Five-second very slow push-in. The warm sidelight opens gently across the report's explanatory areas and focus settles into the paper grain. The report remains flat and physically unchanged; only camera, focus and light move. No new text, values, charts, biomarkers materializing, device-to-report morph, paper flight, particles or cut. Finish with the sheet legible as an object and ample quiet space for the web caption.

## Assembly and review

- Cut or use at most a 6–8-frame optical dissolve between the three clips. Do not turn the whole film into a series of CSS fades; there must be visible, credible camera/material/human motion inside each shot.
- Each direction has one coherent grade. Studio Precision is ivory and neutral; Morning at Home is daylight and wood; Light into Insight is ink and controlled amber. Do not put the exact same image under three color filters and call it three options.
- Default comparison playback is user-controlled. No autoplay under reduced motion. Provide scene selection and readable captions outside the media; if video fails, its generated poster and process text still explain the step.
- Judge: inspect the generated stills and the resulting videos, not prompts alone. Fail a direction for malformed Tasso geometry, tube pointing sideways/up, unnatural skin/hands, independent device motion, unprotected sample return, unintelligible artifacts, fake health outcomes, or missing lab-receipt timing.
- Judge also checks the craft: grounded objects, deliberate frame, restrained camera, coherent light, clear benefit, and three visibly distinct creative directions. Route factual corrections to research, visual/concept corrections to creative and production/interaction corrections to the executor/root. Maximum three review loops remains the limit.
