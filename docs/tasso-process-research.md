# Tasso process research

Reviewed 29 October 2026 for the homepage's three-step collection explanation. The user's latest business instruction is the EVA service brief: home delivery, Tasso collection at home, prepaid return envelope, and results in two working days. Timing begins when the laboratory receives the sample; it does not include delivery or return transit.

## Physical sequence for the animation

The current device instructions establish this order: wash hands and prepare the kit; warm the upper arm, clean the collection area, and let it dry; use a compatible tube with its fill marks visible; remove the protective parts as instructed and adhere the device to the upper arm. Keep the arm relaxed down, with the tube below the device. Press the button fully once, then release it. Collection can take up to five minutes, ending earlier when the top fill line is reached; blood need not appear immediately. Peel off the device, care for the site, detach and fully cap the sample tube, and mix only as specified for that tube. The animation may summarize these actions without becoming a substitute for the included instructions.

For return, identify the sample and collection details according to the kit, protect the capped tube in the supplied return materials, then put the protected sample into the prepaid envelope. The detached, protected sample travels to the laboratory. Do not imply that the entire used Tasso device is mailed back or that a loose tube goes directly into an envelope.

## Three public beats

1. **Collect at home.** Establish that the kit arrives at the person's home, then show the prepared upper-arm placement and a single press/release. Retain a short warming/cleaning cue and direct access to the complete instructions.
2. **Send it back.** Show the protected, sealed sample entering the prepaid return envelope. A schematic envelope is suitable; it need not claim exact delivered packaging.
3. **Understand your results.** Reveal a clear results-ready presentation and the visible qualifier that the two-working-day period starts at laboratory receipt. Show understanding and context rather than a diagnosis or invented personal biomarker values.

## Accuracy and claim boundaries

- This is a marketing explanation, not complete medical-use instructions. Include a calm link to the full manufacturer instructions.
- Keep the device on the upper arm with the attached tube downward. Do not show wrist placement, a finger prick, a sideways tube, instant filling, repeated pressing, or holding the button throughout collection.
- Do not prescribe ten inversions universally. The current device IFU defers mixing to the tube manufacturer's labeling.
- Avoid invented needle-free, universally painless, perfect-sample, and guaranteed-results claims. The device contains a retractable lancet; a product's regulatory status is not approval of EVA's complete testing service.
- Rythm's pickup provider, subscription cadence, biomarker count, U.S. laboratory credentials, and shipping timetable do not transfer to EVA merely because the collection device is shared.
- No new EVA shipping-time, pickup, exact-package, or sample-handling/storage policy was independently verified. Follow the instructions supplied with EVA's actual kit.
