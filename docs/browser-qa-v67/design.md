# v67 instrument design — aligned levels

Impeccable layout/typeset assessment, grounded in v66's actual rendered glass, `.impeccable.md`, current CSS, and the latest direction. The visitor needs to *see the difference in breadth* of two categories against the same sample. A relay of text comparisons no longer meets the request. The following proposes visual encoding; the CMO must confirm the <1 cardiovascular category wording and sources before implementation.

## Spatial thesis

**One specimen, two aligned windows.** Preserve the asymmetric editorial heading, warm paper, smoky purple/amber glass/rim/noise, Hanken 400/600, Georgia italic and dotted sample. Within the instrument put a header with `hs-CRP`, `Example` and a single large dotted `3.2 mg/L` (`3,2` ES). Directly below, a calibrated shared field carries two horizontal tracks in the *same 0–6 mg/L coordinate system*. Their left edges and right endpoints align exactly. Draw a single fine vertical sample guide at 3.2/6 = **53.333%** through both tracks, with a small distinct dot at each crossing. It is a marker of the example, not a destination arrow.

- **Lab track:** label directly above, “This lab’s reference · <5 mg/L” / “Referencia de este laboratorio · <5 mg/L.” A restrained translucent warm-white bar occupies 0–5 (83.333% of shared field); its strict cutoff terminates at an open vertical end marker at 5. Result crossing sits visibly inside this bar. Adjacent conclusion: “Below this lab’s limit” / “Por debajo del límite de este laboratorio.”
- **Lower-risk track:** label directly above, “Lower cardiovascular risk category · <1 mg/L” / “Categoría de menor riesgo cardiovascular · <1 mg/L,” subject to CMO-confirmed wording. A warm amber or pale lavender **filled segment only from 0 to 1** (16.667%); its strict cutoff ends with an open end marker at 1. The result crossing is clearly to its right, on an unfilled baseline. Adjacent conclusion explicitly says the example is above that category, without implying disease or an individual prediction.
- Both tracks share a quiet unfilled baseline extending to 6, and have identical width; bars are deliberately separated vertically, never overlaid into a blended ‘window’. Place 0 and 6 mg/L at the common field edges, with threshold values included in the track labels; avoid overcrowding a mobile axis with 1, 2, 3.2, 5 and 6 all at once. The visual 0–6 is an illustrative display extent, not a biological upper limit. If labelled ticks are added, use only positions that remain readable at 320 and preserve exact scale.
- Put the independently sourced **>2 mg/L if persistent** ESC/EAS review context in the footer as text, not an extra shaded interval or a third competing sample. E.g. “If it stays above 2 mg/L, consider overall cardiovascular risk.” Place the concrete “Repeat when well” action first. Obtain medical/editorial approval of final wording. No shading to the right of 2, no ‘optimal’ label or promise that <1 means universally healthy.

## Craft and hierarchy

Header is compact, instrument field is the visual centre. In the wide glass use approximately 24–28px padding; header → field 20–24px, track groups 20–24px, label → bar 8px, bar → interpretation 8px, footer 20–24px with one hairline. Bars should have a solid readable core (roughly 8–12px high) with subtle inner luminosity and precise squared or lightly rounded endcaps, not a diffuse gradient or glowing health meter. On the 0–1 band even a 10px fill is enough to read as an interval, and its 1/6 width must remain perceptibly shorter than 5/6. The white reference should not overpower the dotted sample; keep the 3.2 readout the largest data object. A restrained dotted calibration pattern is acceptable only if ticks encode actual values; avoid decorative ticks.

The guide should have sufficient contrast against *both* fills and empty track, e.g. a 1–2px warm-white line with contrasting dark halo and a small outlined crossing dot. Do not encode the sample exclusively with colour. Do not make a thick glowing guide that looks like an alert, or use a pointer or gradient suggesting a result is moving. Direct labels and comparison conclusions keep the picture understandable without the disclosure or a legend.

Use Hanken task labels ≥14px/20px and body 16px/24px, existing 400/600 faces only; unit remains readable. Preserve `Example` visibly with the hs-CRP header. Keep meaningful content present before animation; any entrance must be ≤500ms and never animate the value or fills as if a test is being performed. Readout, labels, bar extents, exact strict comparisons and guide require accessible text; decorative drawing should not duplicate screen-reader output.

## Responsive composition

At desktop, maintain the existing left story/right instrument; the header can place identity and large result on opposite ends to leave the *full inner width* for tracks beneath. Tablet around 701–1050 should stack story over a wide instrument, following v66's successful reflow. On 390/320 keep the same two horizontally aligned tracks, with labels and conclusions above/below each track rather than in a side column. Let labels wrap, preserve a single shared drawing width and exact x positions, and let panel height grow. The 320px track still shows a substantial 5/6 versus 1/6 difference; do not replace it with text, truncate the segments, compress font, or make each track a different scale. A single scale caption at the bottom can show `0` and `6 mg/L`; the condition and footer action follow with readable 16/24 type.

## Failure tests for the first render

1. A squint test shows two visibly different interval lengths and the *same* sample crossing both tracks, not a decorative chart with numbers in text only.
2. A cold reader identifies the broad band as this lab's reference and the short band as a lower-risk category; neither the blank region nor the guide appears to be a goal. Test whether the short highlighted band falsely implies a universal target; if so, adjust category label and treatment, not a disclaimer alone.
3. At exactly 1 or 5 an open cutoff remains visually strict, and 2 is not incorrectly presented as part of the <1 category. The actual sample remains fixed at 3.2, never animated or duplicated into competing readouts.
4. Verify EN/ES 320/390/768/1366, zoom and reduced motion; measure painted text and guide contrast on actual glass. Check no labels overlap or get cropped, and confirm source and medical semantics separately. The user requested actual levels: if the two filled lengths are not the first thing distinguishable after the sample, reject the render.
