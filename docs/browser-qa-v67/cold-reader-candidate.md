## Points that gave me pause

- “>2 mg/L if persistent” is compact and slightly circular on first read: it is not clear how persistence is established before repeating the test, or when a repeat should happen. The image gives no timing or explanation of what “well” means.
- “Lower relative heart risk” may sound like a complete assessment of the person's heart risk, even though the nearby caveat narrows it to an hs-CRP category. Being outside this category does not itself say what the person's overall risk is, and the image does not name the category the example falls into.
- The 0–6 scale is below only the second bar, so the shared scale takes a moment to infer, especially on mobile. The threshold numbers are above the right edge of each bar rather than next to their dashed boundary markers. The small dot-and-line markers can be missed.
- The dotted 3.2 is decorative and somewhat hard to read, particularly in the narrow image. The mobile card is dense, with a lot of small text.
- “Interpret your labs” beside “Upload for free” could imply that interpretation itself is free or comprehensive; the image only explicitly says the upload is free and does not explain the service. The headline's suggestion that there is more to “normal” is supported by the two different comparisons, but could also make an in-reference result feel alarming without further context.
