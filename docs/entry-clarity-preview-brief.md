The separate enhanced at-home panel includes ApoB. No other enhanced contents, marker count, cadence, price, timing or availability is inferred. The distinct full laboratory panel retains its already documented €499 one-time price and one year of uploads, with contents and availability to confirm.

Free EVA Score remains conditional on sufficient compatible data. The fictional report can show an existing illustrative derived score without a plan paywall and shows a missing-data state otherwise. This does not implement or validate a clinical scoring algorithm.
