## Why this concept wins

The relay represents a relationship rather than a value scale. A viewer can follow one source into two named comparisons without learning a ruler, dial, target band or probability display. The branches share a common origin, so the result is plainly unchanged; their labels explain why its interpretation differs. The cold-image reader independently recovered the one result, the two cutoffs, the conditional heart-risk review, the repeat-when-well action and the absence of an optimal zone. That evidence supports this selection without proving every possible reader will interpret it identically.

The matrix alternative would regress toward a table on phones. The attached review-slip alternative would demote the review into an optional footnote. The selected relay preserves the equal legitimacy of both questions while retaining one dominant value. Its useful novelty is specific to this communication problem.

## Meaning and medical semantics

- The sole large dotted example remains the visual source, with hs-CRP, example labelling and units attached.
- This laboratory's <5 reference is scoped in its label; the shorter below-limit verdict is now justified by immediate context.

Retain the source disclosure and visible lab-variation/no-diagnosis note. They add context but are not being used to rescue misleading geometry.

## Look, color and craft

The source bay is coherent after metadata/value alignment. The connection meets the sample's central level and the output joints are now clean. Removing the middle rule lets the branch structure organize the comparison groups. The dotted sample is unmistakably the dominant value; ordinary threshold numerals stay subordinate. One deliberate footer rule distinguishes action from interpretation.

Warm paper, restrained amber and the lavender/warm smoked glass remain recognizably EVA. The reflections have atmosphere without mapping to medical outcomes. Fine linework, open terminals and deliberate negative space create a contemporary physical-instrument impression. There is no need to add calibration ticks, a needle, further glow or motion to signal innovation. Such additions would weaken the explanatory clarity.

The cold reader found the dotted glyph and grain less immediately effortless than plain type on a flat surface. This is a candid tradeoff of the pinned identity, not a reason to discard the signature. Here the single dominant value is large, the ordinary text repeats its interpretation, and the connector now has less competing rulework. The remaining visual texture is acceptable in this branded landing context; actual painted contrast still requires its own check.
