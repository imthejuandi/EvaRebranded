## Judgment on the incumbent

V64 has sound core statements and attractive incumbent material, but it is a prose panel rather than the memorable explanatory instrument requested. Its visual hierarchy is header, small equations, sentence, divider, sentence, divider, sentence. The glass becomes a large textured background behind text, with too little diagrammatic meaning. Mobile makes this especially plain: the one number is physically remote from the two readings, and the equations are redundant text rather than a system connecting the sample to two questions. This fails the brief even if all technical and medical checks pass.

## Selected direction in principle

Prefer a **single-sample comparator instrument**: one white dotted 3.2/3,2 mg/L readout visibly feeds two independent labelled comparisons. The geometry represents a relationship — the same sample under two questions — not a concentration axis or a route toward an outcome. It can use an etched fork/connection, two precise comparator apertures or gates, and a restrained amber emphasis attached to the conditional review reading. The connector must terminate at comparison nodes, never at a target zone. This remains provisional until the designer's distinct concepts and actual renders can be assessed.

Do not substitute attractive circuitry for explanation. A line has permission to exist only because it joins this sample to both readings. A gate has permission only if it marks a cutoff comparison. A signal has permission only if its nearby label supplies the meaning. Unlabelled ticks, sweeping needles, segmented arcs, dynamic numerals and generic decoration add cognitive work without useful information.

## Non-negotiable hierarchy

1. The headline and one dominant white dotted result retain the EVA signature. The example label and unit belong to the result, not an unrelated corner.
2. Two questions are visibly distinct, but share one result and one enclosure. Use `Lab reference` / `Referencia del laboratorio` and an explicit heart-risk review label. Avoid two generic feature cards.
3. Each comparison must expose its cutoff and its spoken below/above conclusion. `if persistent` / `si persiste` must sit beside the >2 review cutoff at first glance. The diagram must never assert that this one result has established persistence.
4. A short action follows both readings: repeat the test when well; if still raised, assess overall cardiovascular risk. The lab-variation/no-diagnosis qualification and source disclosure stay readable.

## Strongest copy choices so far

Accept `One result. Two questions.` / `Un resultado. Dos preguntas.` over the current cutoff-first caption. Accept the copy specialist's `Next step` / `Siguiente paso` instead of repeating `Above 2 mg/L?` for a third time. Prefer explicit `Repeat the test` / `Repite el análisis` over a bare repeat instruction. Keep the warm, concise incumbent headline. Do not reintroduce math as the sole explanation.

The Spanish review label is long but truthful; solve it with intentional wrapping. Do not shrink essential labels below 14px to achieve an artificially compact machine face. The supplied body floor remains 16px/24px.

## Color and material judgment

Preserve actual smoked glass, lavender/warm reflections, fine rim/noise and warm paper. These are distinctive brand assets, not defects to normalize. However the useful figure and its labels need enough darker backing that the glass supports reading rather than competing with it. Keep the main result white. Use amber at one semantic locus — the conditional review comparator/its condition — and never as a large filled field, graded region, risk severity scale or flashing alarm. No green/red healthy/unhealthy coding. Do not use matching on/off lamps that imply the laboratory has approved health or the review has diagnosed risk.

## Mobile judgment

The desktop system may use a left-to-right fork; the phone needs a clear compact top-to-bottom equivalent. At 320px the original information order must remain obvious: sample → lab comparison → conditional review comparison → next action. Keep connection geometry out of the text lanes and avoid reserving a wide empty schematic gutter. Do not force both long Spanish labels into tiny side-by-side columns. Preserve useful breathing room; reducing the entire instrument into a dense control board is not premium craft.

## Innovation judgment

The novelty should come from **two independent questions applied to one fixed reading**, expressed as an identifiable physical comparator. That is specific to this educational task and stronger than a generic health gauge. A visually memorable split routing/gate system with restrained analog precision could succeed; a dotted numeral over two prose rows with decorative rules cannot. The finished render must feel intentionally composed, not merely compliant.

## Motion judgment

All evidence must be complete at rest. Optional ≤500ms material settling or one slight group entrance can emphasize the instrument arriving. No line tracing that temporarily withholds the connection, no lights implying persistence has just been confirmed, no numerals counting upward, no needle sweep and no travelling dot suggesting a measured biological process. Normal, calm and reduced-motion views must communicate the identical facts.

## Blocking image failures

- Any area or destination reads as a preferred/optimal target, even if disclosure text denies it.
- Amber reads as a diagnosed risk score or confirmed persistent inflammation.
- The figure requires reading the source disclosure to learn what its shapes mean.
- 3.2 competes with repeated large 3.2 values or secondary cutoffs.
- Mobile demotes the condition, units, sample qualification or next action.
- The central visual is still essentially two paragraphs in glass.

Final judging will select the strongest compatible recommendations and inspect actual EN/ES desktop/mobile images. Passing a detector or counting agent votes will not establish visual acceptance.
