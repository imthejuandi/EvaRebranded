## Specific refinement

Make EVA explicitly explain the difference before introducing history. “Connects” is abstract and does not clearly state the product’s teaching benefit.

Suggested EN revision:

> A lab’s reference range helps interpret a result, but it may differ from levels linked to lower long-term risk. EVA explains the difference and puts your result alongside your health history and previous readings, helping you understand the fuller picture.

Suggested corresponding ES, subject to independent Spain-language signoff:

> El rango de referencia de un laboratorio ayuda a interpretar un resultado, pero puede diferir de los niveles asociados a un menor riesgo a largo plazo. EVA te explica la diferencia y pone tu resultado en contexto, junto con tu historial de salud y tus analíticas anteriores.

Do not add a blanket claim that all laboratory ranges are population averages, all in-range results are suboptimal, or that a single hs-CRP value determines future disease. If explaining reference-population methodology outside the main paragraph, distinguish reference intervals from clinical decision limits and use the CMO’s source-qualified wording.

## Final mechanism-inclusive proposal — text-only verdict

The final copy-specialist proposal includes the reference-population mechanism explicitly:

> Many lab reference ranges describe typical results in selected groups of apparently healthy people. They don’t always identify levels linked to lower long-term risk. EVA explains the difference in plain language and puts your results in context with your health history.

ES:

> Muchos rangos de referencia reflejan resultados habituales en grupos seleccionados de personas aparentemente sanas. No siempre indican qué niveles se asocian a menor riesgo a largo plazo. EVA explica la diferencia con claridad y pone tus resultados en contexto con tu historial de salud.

Verdict: PASS for novice explanation, with no further wording change required. “Many” avoids defining all report limits the same way. The first sentence explains where many reference ranges come from; the second distinguishes that description from lower-risk levels; the third states EVA’s plain-language and context benefit. Spanish preserves those three claims without strengthening them. CMO has passed the exact English. The paragraph does not assert that the particular Valley hs-CRP cutoff is percentile-derived, that the general population is unhealthy, or that all in-range results conceal disease. Its placement still needs to be visibly under the heading, and rendered bilingual/mobile checks remain NOT VERIFIED at proposal stage.
