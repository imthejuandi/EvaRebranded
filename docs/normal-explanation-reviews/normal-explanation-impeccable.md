# Impeccable gates — proposed “normal” explanation

Planning assessment based on actual native v70 EN 1366 and ES 320 captures, the current range section source/CSS, and local Impeccable `typeset`, `layout`, and `craft-floor`. No new copy has been rendered or browser-tested, and this is not medical approval.

## Placement and hierarchy

Place the requested 35–50-word explanation in the existing story column directly below the headline, before the instrument on phones. Make it the primary supporting prose, rather than adding another paragraph next to an equally prominent “One result. Two ranges.” caption. If that caption's meaning is fully carried by the approved new prose, remove the duplicate; otherwise keep it as a short, subordinate bridge. On desktop preserve the asymmetry, with the headline and prose at left and the slim glass instrument at right. Keep the upload action visibly connected to the story without crowding the prose. On phones the order should be heading → explanation → instrument → context/source disclosure → action; the additional height must grow naturally, without clipping or forced viewport fitting.

## Exact type and spacing gates

- Existing EVA Hanken regular 400 for the explanation, **16px / 24px**; use the existing Georgia italic only for the headline. Avoid a new face, synthesized weight, uppercase eyebrow, decorative rule, or text card. Bold only a genuinely necessary short phrase, if any; do not bold whole sentences.
- Start with **16px** from headline to explanation and **24px** between explanation and the next unrelated group; optical adjustments should follow actual renders. Separate two short paragraphs by 8–12px only if the language genuinely has two ideas; do not split every sentence into a list. Do not force a fixed height.
- On desktop cap readable prose around **40–55ch**, within the existing column; do not stretch it across the instrument. The type guidance's 65–75ch ideal is unattainable in this intentionally asymmetric narrow column, and a compact ~35–50-word explanation can remain comfortable at this shorter measure. On 320px use the available column width with natural wraps, not forced line breaks or narrow `max-width` that creates a tall text wall. Use `text-wrap: pretty` as progressive enhancement.
- Preserve the **14px task-label and 16px body floors** in the graphic. Do not shrink text, shorten dot ribbons, reduce the marker, or change exact numeric geometry to recover space. Keep the current separate-lab and lower-risk meanings, the hs-CRP/overall-risk caveat, and the owner-requested absence of the recommendation footer. Do not use emphasis or color that suggests a universal safe or optimal range.

## Content and review limits

Copy and medical specialists own the final wording and claim verification. Each language should read naturally rather than being line-broken to mirror the other. The 35–50-word request is a constraint for the explanation, but it does not mean a single dense block is mandatory: review its natural sentence lengths and visual rhythm. Avoid repeating numeric details already carried by the diagram unless needed to explain it.

Before acceptance, render **actual EN and ES** at 320, 390, 768 and 1366, plus zoom/text scaling. In one batched inspection, assess whether the text feels like a short explanation rather than a massive block; whether the diagram remains the visual centre; whether headline/prose/action spacing and natural wraps work; and whether focus order, links, labels, bounds and contrast remain intact. Check the exact final wording with the medical reviewer and perform a cold read where feasible. An unrendered copy proposal or a clean detector cannot establish those outcomes.

The reviewed EN (41 words) and ES (44 words) versions supplied in the refinement are compact enough to take into a render without a necessary further cut. Set them as one normal-weight 16px/24px paragraph in the outside-glass caption slot; no bold emphasis is necessary. At 46ch on desktop expect a modest multi-line block; at 320 the Spanish will naturally be much taller, so judge its actual rhythm alongside the instrument rather than imposing an arbitrary line cap. If it reads as a wall, first consider a single paragraph break before the EVA sentence or edit the copy with the specialists, not smaller text.
