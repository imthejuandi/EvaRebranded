# EVA refinement: creative briefs and three-pass acceptance

18 September 2026. Planning contribution to the lead's active goal. No application changes in this subtask. These briefs translate the homepage comparison into bounded implementation candidates; they do not declare the redesign complete.

## Direction

Bring the other pages up to the homepage's level of authorship, with a different visual idea for each purpose. Preserve the approved fonts, grain, coral/violet photographic treatment, neutral paper backgrounds and translucent colored glass. Keep information in dark, readable type. Do not import the references' fonts, neon palettes, medical figures or UI wholesale. Do not solve every page with a particle cloud, a centered capsule or a fade-up heading.

Each substantial public page gets one principal composition and one contrasting supporting passage. Each composition must explain something the adjacent copy cannot explain as well alone. Task pages get a distinctive instrument or human detail where useful, while keeping the task immediately available. The number of effects is not an acceptance criterion.

## Public-page briefs

### Method — A sample keeps its identity

**Question:** How does a sample become a report I can use? The preferred candidate is one continuous journey with a persistent illustrative record ID: home context → collected sample/return packaging → laboratory document → readable report. The same identity crosses each transition; the supporting copy explains who performs each step. On desktop the scene can stay alongside natural document scroll. On phone, the relevant scene lives next to each explanation; direct steps remain available.

**Principal assets:** retain the verified Tasso+ product silhouette. Commission a distinct tabletop/home-context image and, only if grounded, a packaging/lab contextual image. Do not invent device anatomy, sample handling or lab equipment procedures. The existing swim image supplies the supporting return-to-life passage. Consolidate the current repeated film/process explanation rather than adding a third version.

**Motion:** crop changes from context to the identified object; that identity becomes a document header, then aligns with the source/date in the report. An original Remotion composition can share that artwork for optional playback, but content must not require watching it. Alternative only if the continuity is unclear: a physical sample/document diptych with direct step selection.

**Pass condition:** a viewer can name home collection, laboratory analysis and EVA presentation as distinct roles, and trace one source through all three. No screen looks like operating instructions invented by EVA.

### Science — Read the evidence, piece by piece

**Question:** What makes a value interpretable? Use a fictional document as the principal visual, then spatially align its name/value/unit, reference, and date/source with an educational reading instrument. Preserve these four anchors throughout. A second paired-document scene explains why dates and compatible units matter.

**Assets:** original paper/report composition and a restrained halftone measurement motif; no new lifestyle photo is needed. Make the comparison composition visually different from the main lens. Numerical examples must come from the current fictional fixture and remain labeled; missing reference stays missing.

**Motion:** marked fields move along short, traceable paths or sharpen into alignment when selected. Grain/halftone provides material, not ambiguous clinical scoring. The direct control and final explanation appear immediately. Retire any decorative particle morph that cannot be described as answering the selected question.

**Pass condition:** the visual demonstrates value plus unit and traceable origin without suggesting a fabricated threshold, diagnosis or improvement. Two-date comparison never interpolates unknown values.

### About — The question behind EVA

**Question:** Why does EVA exist, and what does it choose to do? Keep the new reading portrait as a human opening, then compose an origin passage with smaller artifacts and a focused statement of intent. Use asymmetry and scale change rather than more full-width manifesto text.

**Assets:** current `a-moment-to-read` image; one new non-identifying desk/notebook composition. A real founder portrait or historical artifact is optional only when supplied/verified; a generated person must never stand in as the founder or team. Existing principles become concrete product examples, not four generic icon cards.

**Motion:** a question/annotation gains a corresponding product example, with line/placement continuity. A short photo crop reveal is supporting polish, not the central story. Alternative: a two-image editorial spread if no genuine origin artifact is available.

**Pass condition:** after the opening and origin passage, the visitor can state EVA's purpose in one sentence and identify an actual design/service choice that follows from it. No invented history, quotes, credentials or team.

### Pricing — See what you are choosing

**Question:** What service am I selecting, and what does it include? The main composition has three distinct tangible forms: existing document, at-home kit, laboratory visit/report. Selecting a plan changes the included pieces while price, cadence and CTA remain stable. Keep the service calendar as a secondary explanation of frequency.

**Assets:** original documentary still life of report/paper; verified device/product crop; an abstract reservation/document treatment for a lab visit, avoiding a fictitious partner location. Existing `after-the-morning` photo is the human supporting passage. Do not use the same report photograph as Science's lead.

**Motion:** visible inclusion pieces assemble/disassemble in direct response to plan choice; an anchored selected label explains the relation. Native CSS/React is sufficient. Keep the comparison ledger static and dense. No fake savings, counting prices, computed scores or continuously advancing calendar.

**Pass condition:** a phone visitor sees the plan, price, cadence, main inclusions and action without sitting through a sequence. Illustrative commercial terms remain qualified and match the current plan/entitlement policy.

### Journal — Three ways into understanding

**Question:** Where can I start learning now? Give the three existing learning paths three compact, original editorial illustrations: annotated value/unit, two dated observations, and a methods notebook. A larger photographic pause provides rhythm below; do not pretend this is already a populated magazine.

**Assets:** three coordinated but different vector/paper compositions, plus the existing cycling image. Use Sapiens' hierarchy and concentrated framing as inspiration while retaining EVA type. Do not borrow a SAVEE image as an article cover.

**Motion:** selecting/focusing a path connects its question to its concrete illustration and real destination. Article previews, page turns or issue counts must await actual content.

**Pass condition:** the page feels intentionally designed while clearly saying articles are in preparation. Every featured path opens a real useful page; the demonstration signup remains explicitly non-sending.

### Contact — A clear destination for your question

**Question:** Who/what should receive this message? Keep the form near the opening. Give the routing explanation an authored correspondence composition—an addressed card with the chosen topic and available channel—rather than another decorative capsule.

**Assets:** an original small paper/desk detail can add warmth; use no generated “team” portrait. The form remains the dominant task surface. No large video or pinned scroll.

**Motion:** choosing a topic changes the addressed destination and its explanation without moving the input fields or clearing them. Submission states must describe exactly what the backend/demo did.

**Pass condition:** users can choose the right route, complete the form and distinguish a demo confirmation from a sent message. One compact visual signature is enough.

### Methodology, legal and other public utility routes

Methodology gets a quiet **evidence atlas**: clear navigation from input → model/version → output → limitations, with a single original annotated diagram and source links. The complete document uses excellent typography, anchors and tables; it does not need a film. Models under review remain under review. Legal/privacy/cookies pages use readable measure, prominent dates and section navigation with static content. Beta/signup information makes scope and consent clear. Do not invent compliance badges, editorial imagery or scientific validation to fill space.

## Finite acceptance: exactly three creative passes

### Pass 1 — Select and prove the compositions

For each substantial public page, produce the preferred composition as a working representative section with its immediate readable state, one intermediate state and its final state. At most one alternate candidate per page, only when the primary idea fails the question above. Choose one; do not build a gallery of speculative directions. Task pages use one representative real state and one failure/missing state.

**Gate:** each page has a one-sentence visual purpose, a distinct principal medium/composition, identified source/proposed assets, and a traceable motion relation. Check phone and desktop composition with actual content. A reveal wrapper alone does not satisfy this gate. The independent reviewer must identify what changes and why without reading the implementation plan. Record chosen candidate and reasons. No source licensing or clinical/commercial unknown may be converted into a fabricated asset/claim.

### Pass 2 — Complete routes and test the journeys

Extend the selected language through the real page, remove duplicated narration, and integrate actual state. Review current rendered pages at **390×844 and 1440×900**, plus width/overflow checks at **320 and 820 CSS px**. Test scroll forward/back, direct controls, restored navigation, keyboard-only use, no-JS/default readability where supported, reduced motion at load and toggled mid-session, hidden-tab pause/resume, and lazy loading/failure fallback. Run relevant lint/type and existing functional regressions; add tests only for substantive new logic.

**Hard gate:** no clipped primary information or document-level horizontal overflow; no hidden focusable slides; no focus/page jumps from passive motion; no content blocked by a loading film; no persistent idle animation work offscreen; no mismatched poster/playhead/controls; no broken form/state/access path. Auto motion longer than five seconds has an accessible pause/stop mechanism; reduced motion is static, not merely slower. Values, consent, report identity and entitlement behavior must pass existing regression checks. Root records measurements from actual browser/runtime; source review is not visual certification.

### Pass 3 — One bounded refinement and final verification

An independent reviewer sees the pages beside the homepage benchmark and scores **five dimensions, 1–5**: (1) distinct authorship and fit to purpose; (2) hierarchy/first-use clarity; (3) meaning of visual transitions; (4) material/image/type craft; (5) responsive and interaction coherence. Anchors: 1 broken/incoherent; 2 generic or weak; 3 competent but a visible gap; 4 clearly authored and coherent; 5 exceptional, with evidence. Quiet utility pages can score highly without cinematic motion when that restraint serves the task.

**Creative gate:** every public page scores at least **4 in each dimension**, with a concrete screenshot/interaction observation supporting each score, and no page's principal composition is merely the same object/photo recycled. For each page, fix only the **two highest-impact** remaining creative weaknesses in this final pass. Re-test affected functional paths and confirm every hard gate still passes. Record exact source version, viewport evidence, asset/code provenance and remaining real limits.

After the final review, stop aesthetic experimentation. A demonstrated functional/accessibility regression may receive a targeted repair and retest; it does not reopen art direction or create an indefinite fourth creative pass. If a creative gate remains below threshold, report the specific shortfall and bounded next decision rather than averaging it away or claiming completion. Local source, saved commit and public deployment remain separate states; the lead verifies the exact deployed version before describing anything as live.
