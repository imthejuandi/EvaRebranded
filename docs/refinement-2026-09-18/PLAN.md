## Stages and ownership

1. **Evidence and parity — root + three agents.** Finish original/preview comparison, tool provenance and homepage-to-secondary-page audit. Deliver factual gaps before implementing narrative additions. Preserve baseline commit and audit files.
2. **Creative briefs and acceptance baseline — independent design and test agents.** Specify each page’s purpose, distinct visual scene, motion role, asset source, mobile version and static fallback. Build an executable gate that starts failing and remains failing if required evidence is absent.
3. **Parallel implementation, wave one.** Dedicated public-story builder handles Method/Science; dashboard builder restores source-shaped interpretation and result context in new glass instruments; root art-directs and generates new images, then integrates About/Pricing. Shared files have explicit single owners.
4. **Parallel implementation, wave two.** Journal/Contact receive distinct editorial treatments; authentication/onboarding/upload/report/context/kit/AI/profile/privacy/admin flows receive coherent task-specific refinements. Legal and scientific documents prioritize typography, hierarchy and navigation over gratuitous animation. Each route is explicitly reviewed, including retained decisions.
5. **Independent reviews.** Design review assesses composition against homepage quality; copy review traces claims and actions to original source; motion/art review checks originality, continuity, reduced motion and image consistency. Reviewers report defects, not approval-by-default.
6. **Integration and correction.** Run contract, route, asset, build, responsive, keyboard and motion checks. Fix the largest issues first. Up to three corrective review rounds per page; failed hard gates remain open, never converted into a pass to end the task.
7. **Delivery.** Present the verified local integrated preview, evidence and remaining limitations. Save source history. Public deployment is a separate state and must be verified if requested; this stage does not replace the approved public homepage silently.

## Required acceptance gates

### Business and copy

- Whole-panel summary, per-marker interpretation and generated actions are report-scoped and distinct from static education.
- The most recent ready panel remains readable while a newer sample is pending.
- Critical/unclassified statuses, original/normalized units, missing ranges and estimate provenance survive mapping.
- Saved context is shown accurately. No claim that illness answers influence interpretation until the backend supports it.
- No invented endpoints: existing AI chat is `/api/ai/chat`. Mock adapters remain explicitly disconnected.
- Existing business content and legal/task destinations have a route-by-route disposition; omissions require a reason.

### Real creative/tool delivery

- SAVEE reference IDs must have been visually inspected and mapped to a specific implemented decision.
- At least one appropriate direct 21st component retrieval must lead to a traceable code adaptation actually imported by a rendered route, with source and license recorded. A search result or generic reveal is insufficient.
- New Higgsfield outputs must be visually reviewed, optimized and referenced by their intended pages. An asset sitting in a folder does not count.
- Remotion must have a real visible composition and controls/static fallback. An installed package does not count.
- Long public pages need at least two distinct visual beats, with one dominant scene that communicates their purpose; Contact/auth/legal use task-appropriate exceptions documented in the review.
- Each page must have a purpose-specific composition and asset/motion rationale. Raw effect count is not a quality score.

### Executable checks and human evidence

- Build/type/route checks; report-identity and state-transition tests; provider provenance and asset/import reachability checks.
- Desktop and mobile captures at consistent sizes, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion content availability, no horizontal overflow or hidden primary actions.
- Browser motion measurements identify viewport/environment and timing. No claim of physical iPhone Safari performance from desktop emulation alone.
- Offscreen motion pauses; media has posters/reserved dimensions; meaningful copy remains readable without animation.
- Human design/copy/motion review must cite captures and source version, describe concrete issues, and reach at least 4/5 on composition, hierarchy, visual originality, narrative clarity and consistency. Any serious usability or contract defect overrides the numerical score.

## Completion rule

The goal stays active until the complete integrated scope passes the hard gates and independent reviews. A page can be marked implemented, reviewed, or accepted separately. Never equate a successful build, generated asset, saved commit or local server with a finished design or a public deployment.
