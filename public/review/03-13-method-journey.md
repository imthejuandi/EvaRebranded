# El método — photographic chapters

The method appears before the interactive biomarker example so visitors understand the service before exploring results. Its four steps are home sample collection, laboratory results, interpretation context, and comparison every 90 days.

The visual stage uses EVA’s existing Tasso Glow, runner, stretch and coastal imagery. The warmth, blur and grain belong to those accepted images. The 15 / TÚ / 90 readouts reuse the existing halftone masks and lamp radii. A brief, finite settling animation responds to chapter selection. There are no continuously running charts or videos in this section, and inactive photographs do not load. The surrounding canvas remains warm paper.

References viewed on SAVEE: [_a3PZvq](https://savee.com/i/_a3PZvq/), [Yb1CVRQ](https://savee.com/i/Yb1CVRQ/), [YC6VZQI](https://savee.com/i/YC6VZQI/) and [xMeroxU](https://savee.com/i/xMeroxU/). Their optical softness, glow and sharp foreground readouts informed the treatment.

Tabs support Left/Right, Home/End and visible focus. Previous/next buttons are bounded. All four steps remain in static HTML. Reduced motion removes the finite image and lamp entrances.
